# Daylight Security

> Daylight Security is an AI-native Managed Detection and Response (MDR) provider that combines Agentic AI with elite human expertise to deliver fully customized, mission-aware threat detection and response — end-to-end, in real time. Daylight operates 24x7x365, going beyond triage to take full command of security operations.

Daylight is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 compliant. Founded by Hagai Shapira (CEO) and Eldad Rudich (co-founder), both veterans of an elite IDF intelligence unit and former early employees at security automation company Torq. Daylight has raised $40M in total funding ($7M Seed led by Bain Capital Ventures, $33M Series A led by Craft Ventures with participation from Bain Capital Ventures and Maple VC). Customers include The Motley Fool, Cresta, MIO Partners (McKinsey investment arm), SIXT, Hippo, Telit Cinterion, Burberry, and others.

## Company Mission

Daylight is built for the realities of modern enterprise defense, where true business context, agentic investigations, and elite human expertise are critical to achieving fast, precise, end-to-end threat resolution. The company was founded on the belief that AI can completely redefine what is possible in cyber defense — enabling faster investigations, reducing false positives, and enabling proactive defense at scale, with expert analysts behind it.

The name "Daylight" reflects the company's core promise: always-on, always clear protection that shines a light in the darkest corners of the threat terrain so customers can operate with confidence and peace of mind.

## Service & Platform

### What Daylight Delivers

Daylight's service is called Managed Agentic Security Services (MASS). Unlike traditional MDRs or AI-driven platforms that stop at triage, Daylight delivers true end-to-end detection and response — from real-time detection to full incident resolution.

Core service components:
- **Threat Detection**: Continuous monitoring across endpoints, cloud, and networks
- **Tier 1 Triage**: Automated AI-powered alert triage with near-zero false positives
- **Tier 3 Investigations**: Deep investigations that expose threats others miss
- **Incident Response**: Full containment and neutralization — Daylight takes over from detection to resolution
- **24/7 Expert Support**: Human analysts available at all times, across time zones
- **ChatOps Collaboration**: Direct integration with Slack and Microsoft Teams for real-time collaboration between the customer's team and Daylight analysts
- **Compliance Support**: Helps organizations stay compliant throughout the threat lifecycle

### Key Performance Metrics

- **10x faster** response time compared to traditional MDRs
- **<1 hour** to fully onboard and get operational
- **75% improvement** in Analyst Utilization Rate
- **100% environment coverage** — no blind spots
- **5x MTTR reduction** reported by customers (e.g. Cresta)

### Agentic AI Technology

Daylight's agentic AI goes beyond data collection and normalization. It actively investigates, connects signals, analyzes context, and forms conclusions in real time — mimicking expert reasoning to identify and pursue threats without waiting for human triage. Key capabilities:

- **Autonomous Investigation**: AI agents ingest data, correlate signals, and piece together threat activity in real time
- **Contextual Intelligence**: Learns from business-critical systems (identity platforms, HR systems) to understand roles, relationships, and priorities unique to each customer's environment — even learns from Slack communications
- **Continuous Learning**: Every new deployment, integration, and resolved incident strengthens the platform's collective intelligence, improving accuracy and context awareness over time
- **AI Dashboards**: Full visibility into threats, MTTR improvements, trends, and ROI with actionable, noise-free insights

### Human Expertise Layer

Daylight pairs its agentic AI with PhD-level human analysts who validate findings, handle complex Tier 3 investigations, and collaborate directly with customer security teams. This hybrid model ensures near-zero false positives and eliminates the escalation games common in traditional MDRs.
